---
title: Tips to master graphic design prompts
tags: graphic-design, tips, midjourney
---

- Specify the Medium/Style: Use terms like vector, 3D render, isometric, woodcut line-art, flat design, or Swiss style.
- Define Lighting & Texture: Mention details like frosted glass, screen-printed texture, matte finish, studio lighting, or neon rim light.
- Control Output with Negatives: Use negative prompts (--no in Midjourney/Flux) to exclude unwanted details like photorealistic lighting, complex backgrounds, or garbled text.
